Pengyu An (安鹏宇)

First-year graduate student in Artificial Intelligence, SIGS, Tsinghua University

I am a first-year graduate student in Artificial Intelligence at Shenzhen International Graduate School (SIGS), Tsinghua University under the supervision of Prof. Houde Liu. I obtained my bachelor's degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) and won the Outstanding Graduate Award.

My research interest mainly focuses on robotic manipulation and multimodal fusion. I am aimed to develop robots equipped with generalizable, versatile and robust manipulation capabilities.

During my undergraduate study, I am fortunate to be mentored by Prof. Yonglu Li and Prof. Yuan Qu.

Mitigating The Distribution Shift of Diffusion-based Dataset Distillation

Yue Xu, Chenyu Hu, Pengyu An, and Yong-Lu Li†

The IEEE / CVF Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Conference (CVPR), 2026

paper

In this paper, we introduce a two-stage diffusion-based dataset distillation framework combining Restricted Score Matching (RSM) and Collaborative Guided Sampling (CGS), mitigating training- and sampling-time distribution shifts while preserving sample diversity and achieving state-of-the-art performance with high computational efficiency.

exUMI: Extensible Robot Teaching System with Action-aware Task-agnostic Tactile Representation

Yue Xu, Litao Wei, Pengyu An, Qingyu Zhang, and Yong-Lu Li†

Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL), 2025

project page paper code youtube

In this paper, we introduce exUMI, a portable and extensible robot teaching system with robust proprioception and modular visuo-tactile sensing that achieves near-100% data usability, and propose Tactile Predictive Pretraining (TPP), which learns action-aware, task-agnostic tactile representations from over one million tactile–action-aligned human-play frames through future tactile prediction, capturing contact dynamics, alleviating tactile sparsity, and improving real-world contact-rich manipulation.

Analysis of Low Voltage AC Transmission System with Simultaneous Bi-directional Transmission of AC Power and Signal

Jianfeng Jiang, Zekun Wang, Pengyu An, Zhihang Song, Yihe Shen, Siwen Ye, Xijun Yang, and Jiangdong Mu

International Conference on Energy, Electrical and Power Engineering (CEEPE), 2024

paper

In this paper, we propose a low-voltage AC transmission system that uses full-bridge converters and bipolar voltage modulation to enable simultaneous power and data transfer in either direction over a shared two-wire link, validating its operating principles through simulations and characterizing how transmission-line parameters, switching frequency, transmission distance, and inverter dead time affect output voltage, load capacity, and transmission efficiency.
